Filming the places that take effort to reach.

Northbound Journal started as a way to remember trips, not promote them. It's grown into a weekly-ish record of hiking, van life, diving, and slow travel — filmed by one person with one camera, most of the time.

There's no crew. Most episodes are shot handheld, edited on the road, and published a few weeks after they happen, once there's a real desk to sit at and finish them properly. The goal has never been to make travel look easy — some of the best episodes are the ones where it wasn't.

If you've got a route worth filming or a question about the gear, the contact page reaches a real inbox.
